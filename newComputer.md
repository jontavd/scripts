## Reference

Mac Apps

1. Flux — Adjust screen brightness to nighttime. Partially solved by Night Shift in macOS, but frankly Apple’s version works worse and is way less flexible.
2. iStat Menus — All the info on Mac performance and health
3. TickTick — Menu-bar task manager, super fast and convenient.
4. The Unarchiver — Extracts anything from anywhere, perfect for RAR and other files you get from Windows users.
5. 1Password — Nothing better for storing keys, passwords, credit card infos, and secure notes
6. iMazing — Hassle free sync and file management with iPhone
7. CleanMyMac — Removes extra files, cleans cache, speed up your system
8. Ulysses — Perfect for any kind of writing
9. Mosaic — App for quick switching between apps and windows.
